# L1 account: results (2026-11-06)

Account files: ladder/L1_<arm>/account_s<seed>.py, written by analysis/ladder/l1/build_account.py. One structure for
every seed; the tables are re-derived per seed. Scored by scripts/ladder/l1_accounts.sh, with outputs in
analysis/out/ladder/l1/.

## The account (structure)

1. **Direct path.** The symbol's embedding plus one position vector. Position 0 has its own vector; positions 1-63
   share their mean.
   - Claim (key): the direct path depends only on (symbol, first position or not).
2. **Head.**
   - Score between the current symbol a and an earlier symbol b at position j:
     Σ_r LAM_r[a]·KAP_r[b] + ALPHA[a]·RHO[j]·BETA[b].
   - The first part is three content components. With d_head 4, these are the whole content score table; the
     softmax removes one dimension.
   - The second part is a rank-1 discount of keys near the window start.
   - Output: the softmax-weighted mean of VALUE[b], where VALUE is the value vector at the mean position.
   - Claim (value): the account computes the head's output.
3. **Output.** LayerNorm of (direct + HEAD_OUT · head), scored against the tied embedding.

How it was found (seed 0):
- **Direct path:** the key (symbol, position 0 or not) scores 0.995, and the symbol alone 0.993.
- **Head, content only:** the head with mean positions everywhere scores 0.975. Using the true positions in the keys
  only gives 0.996.
- **Head score table, seed 0:** weighted singular values 17.4, 3.8, 0.36 after row centring. Two components keep
  0.963 of the head's effect, one keeps 0.54.
- **Position effect on keys:**
  - It is mostly rank 1 (weighted R² 0.945).
  - It discounts keys in the first ~16 positions of the window. For a space query and a key of T, the discount is
    -16.8 at j = 0 and -3.2 at j = 8, and about 0 from j = 32 on.
- **Key claims for the head plateau at 0.94-0.946.** The keys tried were:
  - (current, top-scoring symbol): 0.9415;
  - classes of the top two symbols;
  - weight bins.

  85% of the remaining KL is at space queries, which blend several markers.
- **The two-component score failed on seeds 1 and 2** (head 0.877 and 0.914). Their score tables have a real third
  component (seed 1: 22.6, 15.0, 3.6). The account now keeps three components on every seed.

## What this does and does not show

- **L1 ln meets the pass conditions (P1 and P3, all five seeds).** The account is mostly a factorised restatement of
  the weights. The only simplifications are:
  - the mean position for queries, values and the direct path;
  - a rank-1 key-position discount.
- **R1:** the account is about 3 times the size of the weights reference (33-36k bits against 9-12k).
- **The meaning differs by seed** (analysis/ladder/l1/compare_seeds.py). Attention entropy at t >= 16:
  - seed 0: space queries are sharp (0.35 nats) and search for capitals and sentence ends;
  - seed 1: capital queries are the sharp ones (0.49) and space queries are broad (1.77);
  - seeds 2-4: broad everywhere (1.5-3.4). Seed 4's score components are weak (rms 5.6 against 72 for seed 0).

  The docstring's "search for markers" story is seed 0's alone. The shared structure ("content score + early-window
  discount, mean of values") is generic: it is the head's own form.
- **Squeezed bigram (seed 0).** L1's output shape is a tied 4-dimensional bigram after LayerNorm. Fitted directly to
  the true bigram, the best model of that shape reaches 2.638 nats on dev_full. Against it:
  - the model reaches 2.550;
  - the model with the head replaced by its mean given the current symbol reaches 2.597;
  - the true bigram is 2.299;
  - an untied rank-4 bigram with bias would reach 2.388.

  So the tied bottleneck explains why L1 is below the bigram.
- **noln seeds 1-4 fail.** Their position vectors are as large as the embeddings (norm 2.5-6.7 against about 3) and
  vary over the whole window. The key (symbol, t // 8) recovers the direct path to 0.98 on seed 1. The noln account
  needs a position code; not done yet.
